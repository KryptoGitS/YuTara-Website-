Our Startup Story

About YuTara & Team Cri-Ters

Modernizing land discovery and transaction transparency in Panabo City and the Davao Region.

The Local Problem

Fragmented & Unverified Land Listings

In Panabo City and neighboring Davao del Norte municipalities, land buyers and OFW investors often rely on scattered social-media posts and word-of-mouth referrals that lack standardized title disclosures, accurate lot areas, or clear agency coordination.

The YuTara Solution

A Structured Digital Land Marketplace

YuTara centralizes Panabo City property discovery with pre-listing document screening checklists, transparent price-per-sqm metrics, GPS coordinates, and a 5-stage agency-assisted transaction tracker.
