The Local Problem

Fragmented & Unverified Listings

Local buyers and OFW investors often rely on scattered social posts without standardized property records.

  • Unclear land title disclosures and lot measurements
  • Unverified sellers and inconsistent pricing details
  • Manual, disorganized agency coordination
The YuTara Solution

Structured Digital Marketplace

YuTara unifies Panabo City land discovery with transparent records and partner-backed verification.

  • Pre-screened TCT/OCT, Tax Clearance, and Owner IDs
  • Upfront pricing, lot area metrics, and GPS coordinates
  • 5-stage tracked reservation and agency workflow
